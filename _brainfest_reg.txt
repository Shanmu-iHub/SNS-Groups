🔄 ROUND TABLE
1.Two players per team.
2. This game consists of a series of tasks that teams must complete
while rotating through different stations.
Example Tasks:        
🍽️ Eat a dish.
🥤 Stack plastic cups into a pyramid.
🎈 Blow up balloons.
🍾 Flip a bottle and make it land upright.
Gameplay:
                   Each team starts at their assigned table and completes the given task.
          Once finished, they move on to the next table and repeat the process.
                    The game continues until one team successfully completes all tasks.
                    🏆 The fastest team to finish wins the game.
